Fernando van der Kraats is a performer, dancer, and musician associated with the arts scene in the Netherlands and Sint Maarten. Education: He studied at the Amsterdam University of the Arts (Amsterdamse Hogeschool voor de Kunsten / AHK), graduating from its dance department. Performance Work: He has been involved in various theatrical, dance, and musical productions, including collaborations with groups like Untold and Black Harmony, as well as touring as a musician.

Building upon his background in contemporary dance and performance art, Fernando van der Kraats has expanded his footprint into live music and cultural stage productions in the Netherlands. Notably, he has contributed movement and musicality to collaborative theatrical projects such as Uma—produced by Untold and Black Harmony—which explore historical narratives and cultural heritage through dance and percussion. In addition to his stage and theater work, he collaborates as a touring musician and band member, working with artists like singer-songwriter Jason Gwen on live tour projects.

Multidisciplinary Training: Beyond his foundational studies at the Academy of Theatre and Dance (AHK) in Amsterdam, his performance background bridges dance education, physical theatre, and live instrumental accompaniment.Cultural Productions: He has taken part in significant stage projects premiered during events like the Black Heritage Festival at the Bijlmer Parktheater in Amsterdam.

Collaborative Lineups: His work with singer-songwriter Jason Gwen places him in contemporary pop and soul music circles in the Netherlands, contributing to live band configurations and seasonal tours. Stage Identity: Within dance and cultural performance reviews (such as for productions like Uma), he is frequently highlighted for seamlessly blending physical movement, rhythm, and ensemble synchronization to honor historical and storytelling traditions.

Touring and Live Performance: He has been highlighted as a touring band member and musician for projects like singer-songwriter Jason Gwen's album tours. Interdisciplinary Reach: His artistic portfolio bridges multiple creative fields, merging contemporary dance training from Amsterdam's arts institutions with percussion, physical theatre, and modern stage instrumentation.

Additional Stage Appearances: He has performed in cultural dance showcases connected to major Dutch institutional programs, incorporating contemporary physical idioms with traditional African-diaspora rhythms. Broader Creative Network: His professional footprint bridges independent Amsterdam-based performing arts companies and commercial singer-songwriters, positioning him dynamically across both underground theater spaces and mainstream Dutch pop/soul venues.
